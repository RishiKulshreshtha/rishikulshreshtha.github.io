ACCESSIBILITY STATEMENT

This site targets WCAG 2.1 Level AA conformance. That means: full keyboard operability, sufficient color contrast in both light and dark themes, proper landmarks and heading structure, and visible focus indicators throughout.

Known limitation

The five games on the Playground page are visual, mouse/keyboard/touch-driven arcade minigames (two use HTML canvas). They are not operable or meaningful via screen reader, and that's disclosed on that page rather than hidden. Nothing on the rest of the site — About, Projects, Talks, Writing, or Home — depends on them; they're an optional curiosity, not gated content.

Third-party services

This site loads Google Tag Manager (basic page-view analytics) on every page, and the "Say Hello" contact form on the homepage is embedded from Fillout. Neither is used to collect anything beyond standard analytics and whatever you voluntarily submit in the contact form; both are outside this statement's direct control, so their own accessibility and privacy practices may differ from the rest of this site.

Last reviewed

This statement was last reviewed alongside the site's SvelteKit rebuild. If you hit a barrier anywhere on the site outside the Playground page, that's a bug, not a known limitation — please report it.

Reporting an issue

Open an issue on GitHub, or reach out via the contact form on the homepage.
